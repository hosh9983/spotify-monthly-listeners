# One API request covers up to 50 artists, so these 500 take 10 requests a day (about 310 a

# South Korea charts. Each chart's six strongest artists (most and highest-ranked tracks) are
# in, and the rest are the artists that appear on the most charts. Credits that are not
# performers (video games, a film soundtrack, record labels) and one profile with almost no
# listeners of its own were left out.
